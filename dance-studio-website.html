⬡ About The Studio

A Space To
Dance In

Damodar House of Dance is a one-of-a-kind dance studio and creator space for students, artists, choreographers, and content makers. The studio is designed for learning, practice, teaching, shooting, and performance preparation.

Our classes stay structured and welcoming, while workshops and showcases keep the learning alive beyond the weekly batch. From beginners finding rhythm to trained dancers preparing for stage, every mover gets room to grow.

Expert Instructors

Professionally trained, competition-experienced coaches

All Age Groups

Kids (6+), teens, and adult batches available

Creator-Friendly Floors

Spacious rehearsal zones for practice, teaching, and shoots

Workshops & Stage

Guest sessions, dance battles, quarterly showcases, and annual performances

Studio Method

Train Like
An Artist

Every batch follows a clear progression: technique first, expression next, then performance under real studio pressure. Students know what they are building each month and why it matters on stage.

Monthly Progress Reviews Small Batch Coaching Showcase Ready
01

Foundation

Build posture, rhythm, footwork, isolations, stamina, and clean movement habits before choreography gets complex.

02

Expression

Turn steps into choices with musicality drills, freestyle prompts, floor work, and guided improvisation.

03

Performance

Rehearse entries, formations, stage presence, camera confidence, and cypher etiquette for real audiences.

⬡ Meet The Team

Your
Coaches

🕺
Arjun Damodar
Founder & Head Coach — Hip-Hop

With over 14 years of street dance experience and a decade of competitive battling across South Asia, Arjun brings raw energy and technical precision to every class. Trained in breaking, popping, and locking, he's competed at Red Bull BC One qualifiers and mentored three national-level champions.

Hip-Hop Breaking Battle Prep
💃
Priya Menon
Senior Instructor — Contemporary

Priya trained at the Chennai School of Arts and spent three years with a contemporary dance company in Mumbai before returning to teach. Her classes blend somatic technique, floor work, and improvisation, helping students develop a deeply personal movement vocabulary.

Contemporary Fusion Kids
🎵
Kiran Raj
Youth Coach — Groovezone

Kiran specialises in making dance accessible and fun for younger students aged 5–15. His high-energy Kids Groovezone sessions use games, rhythm challenges, and freestyle circles to build confidence alongside coordination. Parent favourite for three consecutive years.

We assess where you are in your free trial class and place you in the right batch. Your first class at DHOD is completely free with no obligation. It's a full-length session — not a taster — so you'll get a real feel for the coaching style, the energy of the batch, and whether it's the right fit for you. Just fill in the enquiry form and we'll schedule it around your availability.
Wear comfortable, breathable clothing that gives you full range of motion — track pants, joggers, or shorts work well. For footwear, clean indoor sneakers or dance shoes are recommended (avoid slippers or hard-sole shoes). Bring a water bottle; we have changing rooms but no lockers, so travel light.
Fees are charged monthly and are due on the 1st of each month. We accept UPI, bank transfer, and cash. The monthly fee covers all classes within your chosen batch for that month. Workshop Series and one-off masterclasses are priced separately. There is no registration fee beyond the first month's payment.

Monthly fees are non-refundable once the month has started. If you need to take a break, you can freeze your membership for up to 30 days once per year — fees don't apply during the freeze.
