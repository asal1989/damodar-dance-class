Quick Answers

FAQs

Short answers for students and parents before they book a demo class.

Do I need prior dance experience?

No. Beginner batches are designed for absolute beginners, and we place students in the right batch after the demo class.

What should I wear?

Wear comfortable clothing and clean indoor sneakers or dance shoes. Bring a water bottle and travel light.

How do I get directions?

Call or WhatsApp +91 78923 10080. The studio is beside Ganesh cycle shop in Hosapalaya, Muneshwara Nagar.
